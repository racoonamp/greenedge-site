GreenEdge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short: GreenEdge doesn't collect any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud account. There are no accounts, no servers, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores

GreenEdge stores the information you choose to enter: your day ratings, notes, trackers and the options you log, plus a few settings (such as which day your week starts on).

Where it's stored

Your data is stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, it is also synced through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so it's available on your other devices. This iCloud data is private to your Apple Account: the developer of GreenEdge cannot see or access it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we collect

Nothing. GreenEdge has no servers and no accounts, and it contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. The app makes no network connections other than Apple's iCloud sync.

Tracking

GreenEdge does not track you across apps or websites, and doesn't share data with anyone.

Exports

If you choose to export a backup (JSON) or a spreadsheet (CSV), the file is created on your device only when you ask for it, and goes wherever you choose to send it. Temporary export files are deleted after sharing.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete all data. With iCloud sync on, this also removes your data from iCloud and your other devices. Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.

Children

GreenEdge doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
