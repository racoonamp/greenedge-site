GreenEdge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: GreenEdge doesn't collect any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud account. There are no accounts, no servers, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores

GreenEdge stores the information you choose to enter: your day ratings, notes, trackers and the options you log, plus a few settings (such as which day your week starts on).

Apple Health

GreenEdge can show information from Apple Health beside your days, if you choose to connect it. It asks only for the kinds you turn on, and only to read them: weight, sleep, steps and exercise minutes, resting heart rate and heart rate variability, calories eaten, mindful minutes, waist and body fat, and cycle tracking. It reads from the day before your first day in GreenEdge (rated, noted or logged); cycle tracking from about two months before that, to place your first cycle; and every weigh-in you have. GreenEdge never writes to Apple Health.

What it reads from Apple Health is used on your iPhone to work out what's shown in the Health tab, and for nothing else. It is not saved in GreenEdge's own data, not synced to iCloud by GreenEdge, not included in backups or exports, and never leaves your device. It is not used for advertising or shared with anyone.

You can stop this at any time: remove GreenEdge's access in the Health app (your picture → Apps → GreenEdge), or, while you have Premium, turn a kind off in GreenEdge's Settings → Apple Health. Without Premium the Health tab is locked and nothing is read.

Purchases

GreenEdge Premium is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; GreenEdge never sees your name, card or Apple Account details. The App Store tells the app only whether Premium is owned, and that stays on your device.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, it is also synced through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so it's available on your other devices. This iCloud data is private to your Apple Account: the developer of GreenEdge cannot see or access it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we collect

Nothing. GreenEdge has no servers and no accounts, and it contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. The only network connections the app makes are to Apple: iCloud sync, and the App Store, which tells the app which version of it you have and handles purchases. The privacy policy link in Settings opens this page, which is hosted on GitHub Pages, in a Safari view inside the app.

If you write to us from Settings → Contact support, the email you send comes to the developer with your email address and, filled in for you, the app version, iOS version and device type, to help with your question. It is used only to reply, and you can delete those lines before sending.

Tracking

GreenEdge does not track you across apps or websites, and doesn't share data with anyone.

Exports

If you choose to export a backup (JSON) or a spreadsheet (CSV), the file is created on your device only when you ask for it, and goes wherever you choose to send it. Temporary export files are deleted after sharing.

A week, month or year can also be shared as a picture of its colours. If you tap Save to Photos, GreenEdge asks only to add that picture to your photo library: it can't see or read your photos.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your ratings, notes and trackers at any time in Settings → Your data → Delete all data. With iCloud sync on, this also removes them from iCloud and your other devices. Your settings (such as your week start and goals) are kept. Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.

Children

GreenEdge doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
