How does iCloud sync work?

If you're signed in to iCloud, GreenEdge syncs automatically through your private iCloud account. You can check the status in Settings → iCloud sync. If iCloud isn't available, your data is saved on your iPhone and syncs when iCloud is back.

How do I back up or move my data?

Settings → Export backup (JSON) saves everything. Settings → Restore from backup merges a backup into your data; where a day exists in both, the most recently changed version is kept. Restoring the same file twice is safe.

Can I fill in past days?

Yes. Use the arrows or tap the date on Today, or tap any day in the Calendar. If yesterday isn't rated yet, Today offers a shortcut to it.
