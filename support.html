How does iCloud sync work?

If you're signed in to iCloud, GreenEdge syncs automatically through your private iCloud account. You can check the status in Settings → Sync and backup. If iCloud isn't available, your data is saved on your iPhone and syncs when iCloud is back.

How do I back up or move my data?

Settings → Your data → Export backup (JSON) saves every day, note and tracker (not your settings). Settings → Your data → Restore from backup merges a backup into your data; where a day exists in both, the most recently changed version is kept. Restoring the same file twice is safe.

Can I fill in past days?

Yes. Days you haven't rated show blue in the Calendar. Tap any day to rate it.

How does Apple Health work with GreenEdge?

GreenEdge reads from Apple Health; nothing is typed into GreenEdge itself. Your scale, watch or another app saves to Apple Health, and GreenEdge shows it beside your days. Connect it in the Health tab or in Settings → Apple Health. If nothing shows, open the Health app → your picture → Apps → GreenEdge and check it's allowed to read what you want. GreenEdge only reads, and what it reads stays on your iPhone.

GreenEdge Premium: buying, restoring and cancelling

Premium unlocks the Insights and Health tabs; everything else is free. Buy it from either tab or from Settings → GreenEdge Premium. On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases there. A subscription is cancelled in your App Store account: Settings → GreenEdge Premium → Manage subscription, or the App Store → your picture → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
